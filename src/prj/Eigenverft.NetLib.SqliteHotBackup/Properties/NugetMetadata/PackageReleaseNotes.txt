Add Automatic (default) and Manual discovery modes, scoped dynamic file-source providers and the thread-safe process-local ISqliteBackupTargetRegistry. Manual skips EF discovery and automatic provider resolution. Configured and runtime files participate in both modes. Inventory and execution acquire fresh detached snapshots. Source-query failures retain partial inventory and do not suppress healthy backups/archives; inspect SqliteBackupDiscoveryException or SqliteBackupRunResult.DiscoveryFailures. Database creation, readiness, catalog persistence and scheduling remain consumer-owned.

Retains the initial SQLite online-backup behavior for .NET 8 and .NET 10: stable target IDs, all/single/selected calls, per-source progress-stall limits with global inheritance and ID overrides, safe no-overwrite publication, best-effort execution, optional ZIP/raw cleanup, and opt-in shutdown WAL checkpoints. No Hangfire dependency or consumer migration is included.

Uses EF Core SQLite 8.0.34/10.0.15 and Hosting.Abstractions 8.0.1/10.0.15 for the .NET 8/.NET 10 targets.
